About Me

I am an undergraduate engineering student pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Biomedical Engineering at Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering and Technology (DSCET), Mamallapuram. My academic journey combines core biomedical instrumentation concepts with an energetic and hands-on dedication to hardware prototyping, PCB layout design, and embedded systems.

DEVELOPING PRACTICAL ENGINEERING SKILLS Rather than confining engineering to theory, I actively build hardware prototypes. From drafting schematics and routing traces in KiCad to breadboarding sensor circuits with the ESP32 and through-hole soldering physical PCBs, I am dedicated to learning through direct bench experimentation.

Serving as an Innovation Ambassador at DSCET, I engage with technical competitions, hackathons, and hardware challenges that require bridging interdisciplinary fields: turning physiological signals into actionable embedded data, developing safety-critical monitoring layers, and creating assistive mechanisms designed with the patient in mind.

// MY PROTOTYPING WORKFLOW: FROM CONCEPT TO COPPER
PHASE 01
Problem & Schematic
Analyze biomedical requirement, select components, simulate timing/amplification in KiCad / Tinkercad.
PHASE 02
Breadboard Proof
Wire discrete ICs and sensors to confirm pin logic, signal levels, and baseline response.
PHASE 03
PCB Layout
Route 2-layer traces in KiCad, verify DRC clearances, place ground copper pours and silkscreen.
PHASE 04
Assembly & Solder
Hand solder through-hole components, inspect joints for solder bridges, verify power rails.
PHASE 05
Firmware & Test
Deploy ESP32 / microcontroller logic, acquire real signals, and validate system stability.
